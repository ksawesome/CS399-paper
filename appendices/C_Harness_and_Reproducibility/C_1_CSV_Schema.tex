\subsection{Harness Output CSV Schema}\label{app:csv_schema}

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Fields captured in the harness per-call CSV output.
\label{tab:csv_schema}}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
	oprule
	extbf{Column Header} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Description} \\ \midrule
	exttt{run\_id} & string & Unique run identifier for provenance tracking. \\
	exttt{date\_time} & ISO-8601 UTC & Timestamp recorded when the API response was received. \\
	exttt{seed} & integer & Random seed applied for sampling or shuffling the prompt set. \\
	exttt{prompt\_id} & string & Stable identifier that ties the row back to the prompt bank. \\
	exttt{prompt\_category} & string & Topical category associated with the prompt. \\
	exttt{model} & string & Provider/model name reported by the harness. \\
	exttt{model\_version} & string & Provider-supplied version tag for the endpoint. \\
	exttt{latency\_ms} & number & End-to-end response latency measured in milliseconds. \\
	exttt{tokens\_in} & integer & Input token count submitted to the API. \\
	exttt{tokens\_out} & integer & Output token count generated by the model. \\
	exttt{cost\_usd} & number & Calculated per-call cost (see \cref{eq:per_call_cost}). \\
	exttt{response\_length} & integer & Character length of \texttt{response\_text}. \\
	exttt{response\_text} & string & Sanitized model output retained for human review. \\
	exttt{http\_status} & integer & HTTP status code returned by the provider. \\
	exttt{error\_code} & string? & Optional standardized error code supplied by the API. \\
	exttt{error\_message} & string? & Optional diagnostic message associated with \texttt{error\_code}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Notes: (i) \texttt{response\_text} is subject to the sanitization hook in \cref{subsec:sanitization_procedures}; (ii) price tables used to compute \texttt{cost\_usd} are recorded in run metadata and referenced in \cref{sec:cost_modelling}.
